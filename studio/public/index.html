ToonFlow User Agreement

Version 1.0 · Effective when you accept during signup.

Your account and work

Keep your login secure and provide accurate account information. You retain your rights in the stories, artwork, references, and films you upload or create. Only upload material you own or have permission to use.

AI-assisted creation

When you use the Codex director, your project text and selected reference images are sent to the configured AI service to carry out your instruction. Review generated scripts, animation, and synthetic voices before using or publishing them.

Saving and publishing

Projects, profile answers, and render progress are stored in the website database. Media files are stored by the studio server. Your films remain private until you choose Publish. Publishing makes the film and its title, genre, theme, and creator name available through its public watch page. Do not publish personal information about others without permission.

Optional questions

Onboarding answers personalize your workspace. Questions may be skipped. These answers and accepting this agreement do not opt you into marketing messages.

Service availability

This is an early studio release. Rendering depends on available tools and server capacity. Keep downloadable backups of important work. Features and this agreement may change; a new agreement version may require acceptance.

Responsible use

Use the studio lawfully. Do not attempt to access another account, misuse credentials, or impersonate someone through synthetic voices without appropriate permission.
