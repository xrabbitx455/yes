# RED vs. BLUE: FRONTLINES
## SEASON 1 — EPISODE 1: FIRST CONTACT

**Genre:** Military Action / War Drama  
**Runtime:** Approximately 7 minutes  
**Animation Style:** 2D Military Stick Figure Animation  
**Resolution:** 1920 × 1080  
**Frame Rate:** 24 FPS

---

# 1. SERIES OVERVIEW

Two rival military factions, the Red Forces and Blue Forces, are fighting over control of a fictional country. Both factions have professional soldiers, armored vehicles, helicopters, military bases, and command structures.

The series follows the soldiers fighting on both sides of the conflict. Combat is grounded, cinematic, and dangerous. Soldiers can be injured or killed, ammunition is limited, and military equipment behaves realistically.

There are no supernatural abilities, exaggerated acrobatics, or instant respawns.

Episode 1 introduces the conflict through a small Blue military patrol sent to investigate an abandoned border checkpoint.

What begins as a routine reconnaissance mission escalates into the first major engagement of a new war.

---

# 2. MAIN CHARACTERS

## BLUE-1 — SERGEANT MASON
**Role:** Patrol Leader  
**Weapon:** M4 Carbine  
**Personality:** Calm, experienced, disciplined, decisive.

Mason commands the four-man Blue patrol. He prefers avoiding unnecessary engagements but will protect his soldiers at all costs.

## BLUE-2 — CORPORAL HAYES
**Role:** Radio Operator  
**Weapon:** M4 Carbine  
**Equipment:** Military radio and communications backpack.

Hayes handles communication with headquarters. He is professional but becomes nervous when situations deteriorate.

## BLUE-3 — PRIVATE JACKSON
**Role:** Automatic Rifleman  
**Weapon:** M249 Light Machine Gun  
**Personality:** Confident, outspoken, occasionally humorous.

Jackson provides the squad's primary automatic firepower. He is eager to prove himself but sometimes acts impulsively.

## BLUE-4 — SPECIALIST REED
**Role:** Combat Medic  
**Weapon:** M4 Carbine  
**Equipment:** Medical backpack and first-aid supplies.

Reed is the youngest member of the patrol and has never experienced actual combat.

## RED PATROL LEADER
**Role:** Red Infantry Patrol Commander  
**Weapon:** AK-pattern Assault Rifle

A disciplined Red soldier commanding a three-man patrol near the border. He believes the territory belongs to the Red military and is unwilling to abandon his wounded men.

# 6. CAMERA DIRECTION

Use cinematic camera movement while maintaining the 2D animation style.

**Wide Shots:** Establish the environment, battlefield geography, and troop positions.

**Medium Shots:** Show soldier movement, dialogue, and interactions.

**Close-Ups:** Highlight weapons, radio equipment, wounded soldiers, and important reactions.

**Over-the-Shoulder Shots:** Build tension during confrontations.

**Tracking Shots:** Follow soldiers walking, running, or vehicles driving.

**Combat Shots:** Use controlled camera shake, fast cuts, and environmental impact effects.

Avoid excessive zooming or camera shake that makes the action difficult to follow.

# 10. EPISODE ENDING AND CONTINUITY

Episode 1 ends with the revelation that the Red military has begun a large-scale invasion.

The Red armored column is advancing toward their position.

The unresolved mysteries are:

- What happened to the original Echo-7 garrison?
- Who fired the first shot?
- Why was the Red military preparing an invasion?
- Will the Blue patrol escape before the armored column arrives?
